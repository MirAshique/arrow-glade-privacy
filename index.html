Privacy Policy for Arrow Glade: Tap Escape

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Arrow Glade: Tap Escape ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is developed and published by Mir Hussain. This policy applies to the App as distributed on Google Play.

1. Information the App Collects Directly

The App does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. We do not knowingly collect personal information through gameplay itself.

Your game progress and preferences—including completed levels, stars, coins, hint credits, daily challenges, streaks, garden progress, unlocked themes, and sound or other settings—are stored locally on your device. This gameplay data is not sent to our own servers. It is generally removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Data

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. Ads may appear as banners, full-screen ads between eligible levels, or optional rewarded ads that offer a hint credit. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information to deliver, secure, and measure advertising, including:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, where available and permitted
  • General device and network information, such as device type, operating system, IP address, and approximate location inferred from IP address
  • Ad interactions, app activity related to advertising, and diagnostic or performance information

Google handles this information according to its policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

For users in regions where advertising consent or privacy choices are required, including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent or present relevant privacy choices as applicable. Whether and which message is shown depends on applicable requirements and Google's consent system.

Where required, you can review or change your choices using the Privacy Options control in the App's Settings. The App's offline gameplay remains available even if ads cannot be requested.

4. Advertising ID Controls

Depending on your Android version, you can manage or delete your device's advertising identifier through your device settings, generally under:

  • Android: Settings → Google → All services → Ads (the exact path may vary by device)

5. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through gameplay. If you have a concern about a child's privacy, please contact us using the email below.

6. Data Security, Retention and Deletion

Locally saved gameplay data is kept on your device until you remove it, subject to your device's backup and restoration settings. You can remove it by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. We do not operate player accounts or a cloud game-save service.

Information processed by Google AdMob and its partners is subject to their own security, retention, and deletion practices. Adjusting device advertising settings and UMP privacy choices may affect future advertising data processing; these controls do not necessarily delete data already retained by third parties.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, its services, or applicable requirements. We will post an updated policy on this page and revise the "Last updated" date.
