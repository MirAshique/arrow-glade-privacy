This policy explains how Arrow Glade: Tap Escape (the game) handles information. The game is developed and published by Mir Hussain. It is an offline-first puzzle game: campaign play and local progress do not require an internet connection.

1. Local gameplay information

No player account is required. The game does not ask for your name, email address or phone number to play, and it has no cloud-save service.

Progress and preferences are saved in private storage on your device. These include completed levels, stars, puzzle attempts, coins, hearts, hints, reward receipts, Daily Challenge results and streaks, Garden progress, unlocked themes, and sound and other settings. The game code does not upload these saves to our servers. The gameplay save is stored in Android's no-backup area and excluded from Android automatic backup.

Local gameplay storage is separate from information handled by Google's privacy and advertising services. Offline-first does not mean that every third-party service operates offline or processes no information.

2. Intended audience and local age-range choices

The intended worldwide audience is ages 13–15, 16–17 and 18+. Children under 13 are not the intended audience. These audience groups are separate from the game's Google Play content rating.

The game offers a neutral, optional age-range choice: Under 13, 13–15, 16–17, 18+, or Prefer not to say. You can also continue without choosing. No answer unlocks gameplay or earns a reward; all gameplay remains available regardless of your answer.

This feature stores only your current coarse choice, locally in Android's private no-backup area, separate from your gameplay save. It does not collect an exact date of birth, an account, an identity document, a selection history or a selection timestamp. The game code does not upload or log your age choice. It is not age verification, proof of identity, consent to data processing, or permission to show ads.

To correct your choice, open Settings → Age range and privacy. To remove a saved age range, use Remove age range in Settings. Removal replaces the range with an unspecified-choice marker so the game can remember that no range is specified. It does not erase gameplay progress or change Google's separately managed privacy choices.

3. Current advertising status

Production advertising is currently disabled for everyone pending compliance approval, including applicable regional privacy, age-assurance and consent review. No production banner, interstitial or rewarded ad is requested through the current advertising flow.

Google AdMob integration is prepared for possible future eligible use. In V1, under-18 users and users whose age is unknown are not eligible for production ads. Selecting 18+ does not by itself establish eligibility or enable advertising.

If advertising is enabled for eligible users in a future approved configuration, the prepared formats include banners, full-screen ads at eligible level boundaries, and voluntary rewarded ads offering a hint credit. Privacy choices alone do not override the game's separate advertising restrictions. Ordinary offline gameplay does not depend on watching an ad.

4. Google consent and privacy choices

The game integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for official consent and privacy-options forms. This includes support for European regulations affecting the European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland, and applicable US state privacy regulations.

Privacy processing is separate from requesting ads. In the current production configuration, new UMP consent-information requests remain blocked for all local age selections until the necessary age and regional processing review is complete. An age selection does not authorize an unrestricted consent request. Under-age-of-consent protection is handled separately from advertising eligibility.

When UMP reports that a privacy-options entry point is required, Settings → Privacy Choices provides access to Google's form. Available options may allow you to review, change or withdraw consent, or make applicable US sale/sharing and advertising choices. Existing choices remain managed by Google; the game does not synchronize consent across different apps.

A form or Settings entry is not guaranteed to appear. Availability depends on permitted processing, region, age treatment, Google's status, configured and published messages, and network availability. Where a new information request is blocked, the game may be unable to discover the applicable choices on a fresh launch. Contact us below if you need help with a privacy request or cannot access an option.

Privacy-service errors and unavailable forms do not prevent offline puzzle play. In a future eligible advertising flow, declining personalized advertising would not necessarily stop all forms of advertising or all SDK processing. Production ads remain disabled under the current configuration regardless of any consent response.

Android also provides advertising identifier controls, including reset or deletion where supported. Look for Ads under Google services in your device's Settings; names and availability vary by Android version and device.

5. Third-party services and information processing

Google's privacy services can use network connections and process consent choices and related technical information to determine and manage applicable messages. When a permitted UMP request or form communicates with Google, that processing is separate from local game saves and does not itself mean an ad has been requested.

If Google Mobile Ads is activated for eligible advertising, Google's SDK disclosure describes collection and sharing of information including:

  • IP address, which can be used to infer approximate location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics and performance information.
  • Device and account identifiers, including Android advertising ID and app set ID, where available and applicable.

Google describes advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as purposes for this SDK processing. Actual processing depends on the service, configuration, device and applicable privacy settings. These advertising SDK categories are not a claim that all such data is currently transmitted while production advertising is disabled.

Google handles information under its own policies. If future ads lead to another website or service, that provider's policies apply there. Local-only age storage and disabled ads do not establish that the whole app has no third-party data processing. Google Play's Data safety disclosures must account for the actual released configuration and SDK behavior, separately from this policy.

6. Retention, deletion and your requests

Local gameplay information remains on your device until you clear the game's storage, uninstall it or lose that storage. Your age-range choice remains until you change or remove it, clear app storage, or uninstall. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local progress, settings and earned items as well as the local age choice. Without a cloud save, the game cannot restore these from a server.

You can remove local data through Android Settings by opening the game's app information and choosing Clear storage or Clear data, or by uninstalling it. To change only your age choice without losing progress, use the Settings controls described above. There is no player account to delete.

Google manages retention and deletion of information it processes under its policies. Clearing local game data, changing an age range, or changing privacy or advertising settings does not delete information already held by Google or necessarily reset every preference.

Depending on where you live and the information involved, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to or limit certain processing. Contact infocodefluxdev@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests, including concerns about a child's information. Do not send identity documents or an exact date of birth to use the game's age-range feature.

If you email us, your email address and the information you choose to include are received as correspondence to address your inquiry. This is separate from gameplay and is not collected by the age-range feature. You may use the same contact to ask about retention or deletion of that correspondence. We cannot retrieve device-only saves from a server or promise to delete information held independently by Google.

7. Security

Android's app-private storage restricts ordinary access to local saves by other apps. The game uses atomic file replacement to reduce incomplete writes; this is not a guarantee against device loss or damage. Google's Mobile Ads documentation describes TLS encryption for its data in transit. No storage or transmission method guarantees complete security.
